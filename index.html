Should You Switch to Time-of-Day?

TOD charges $0.31826/kWh on-peak (weekdays 2–7pm, Jun–Sep) but only $0.08638–$0.11247/kWh everywhere else — well below the Standard Rate's $0.10897–$0.13897. Model what happens if you also shift some on-peak habits to off-peak hours.

Move dishwashing, laundry, pool pumps, and EV charging to before 2pm or after 7pm on summer weekdays — or pre-cool the house before 2pm so the AC coasts through the on-peak window.
